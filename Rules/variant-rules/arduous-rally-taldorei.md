# Arduous Rally
*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 118* 

The party spends 5 minutes to rally their resolve in the face of coming peril, the adrenaline fueling their need to push on. The party benefits from the completion of a full Short Rest, but all healing gained by spending hit dice is halved, and all rallying characters suffer 1 point of [Exhaustion](Rules/conditions.md#Exhaustion) (p. 291 PHB).
