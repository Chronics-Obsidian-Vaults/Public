# Familiars
*Source: Volo's Guide to Monsters p. 213* 

Any spellcaster that can cast the [find familiar](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/find-familiar.md) spell (such as an apprentice, warlock, or wizard) is likely to have a familiar. The familiar can be one of the creatures described in the spell (see the "Player's Handbook") or some other Tiny monster, such as a [cranium rat](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/aberration/cranium-rat-mpmm.md), a [crawling claw](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/undead/crawling-claw.md), a [gazer](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/aberration/gazer-mpmm.md), an [imp](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/fiend/imp.md), a [pseudodragon](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/dragon/pseudodragon.md), or a [quasit](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/fiend/quasit.md).

> [!note]
> This variant is intended for NPCs. (https://twitter.com/JeremyECrawford/status/920714594334654465)
