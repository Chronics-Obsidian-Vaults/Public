# Multi-Spell
*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 118* 

At around 8th level, gift the [Spelldriver](Compendium/feats/spelldriver-taldorei.md) feat to your players. This will allow spellcasting classes more options to aid themselves and the party at higher levels without a comparably sluggish per-round ability set, spending their solitary action on a bonus action spell. This would also apply to enemy spellcasters, leading to additional chaos and challenge.
