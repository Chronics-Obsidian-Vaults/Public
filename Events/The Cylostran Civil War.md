The Cylostran Civil War, commonly known as The Rending, erupted in the year 3228 of the Age of Flourish. At this time, [[Cylostra|Cylostra]] existed as a unified continental nation called the Cylostran Empire, with its capital city established in [[Kaskond|Kaskond]].

Official imperial records attribute the war's outbreak to territorial disputes over the [[Alderian Expanse]], the vast rolling plains that form the heartland of the continent. However, alternative accounts suggest a more calculated origin—a possible orchestrated plot to frame the eastern city of [[Amberlight]] as an illicit weapons supplier to [[Lyranthe|Lyranthe]]. This theory gained credibility following the sudden appearance of numerous Amberlit vessels in Lyranthe's harbours, delivering substantial quantities of military equipment in the months preceding the conflict.

## Duration and Calendar Significance

The war raged for 27 devastating years, reshaping the continental landscape both physically and politically. Following Cylostran tradition, where major historical events reset the calendar, this period was designated as the [[Rending Era]] (RE), marking the beginning of a new historical epoch.

## Early Conflict Zones

The initial years of the war saw concentrated fighting across the western territories between Lyranthe and Kaskond. These early skirmishes and battles claimed tens of thousands of lives according to imperial records, though independent verification of these casualty figures remains elusive.

## Strategic Approaches

### Kaskond's Strategy

The imperial capital adopted warfare methods heavily reliant on subterfuge and intelligence operations. Central to this approach was an elite organization known as the [[The Emperors Talons|The Emperor's Talons]], a specialized college of soldiers funded directly by imperial coffers. These operatives excelled in stealth, espionage, counter, intelligence, and targeted assassination, striking at enemy leadership and infrastructure rather than engaging in conventional warfare.

> "An Emperor's Talon requires no banner, no armour, no oath spoken aloud. Their loyalty is written in the blood of the Empire's enemies, their victories recorded only in the silence that follows."
> 
>  — Attributed to the Imperial Spymaster

### Lyranthe's Defence

The southern city, state implemented a radically different strategy cantered on isolation and territorial expansion. Leveraging its considerable magical expertise and engineering capabilities, Lyranthe constructed the massive Ice Wall, completely severing land connections with the rest of Cylostra. Simultaneously, the city's powerful mages engaged in unprecedented climate manipulation, freezing vast portions of the southern sea to create additional territory for expansion and defence.

### Amberlight's Neutrality

The eastern city of Amberlight maintained neutrality throughout the conflict, benefiting from natural geographical barriers including the Exalted Peak and challenging surrounding terrain. This position allowed the city to avoid direct military confrontation through much of the war.

## Collateral Consequences

### Impact on Amberlight

Despite its neutrality, Amberlight suffered severe consequences from both warring parties' strategies:

The Emperor's Talons conducted widespread sabotage operations within Amberlight, targeting guilds, assassinating political leaders, disrupting trade routes, and fostering civil unrest that led to escalating crime rates.
Lyranthe's magical manipulation of the southern seas caused rising water levels that progressively eroded Amberlight's coastal regions. Persistent flooding eventually submerged nearly half the city over the course of the war.

## The Battle of the Thousand

The war reached its cataclysmic conclusion in the 24th year of the Rending Era when Lyranthe's Shining Army marched eastward toward Amberlight. Upon learning of this manoeuvre, Kaskond mobilized the Imperial Army in response, also moving eastward.
Soon both forces surrounded the Exalted Peak. Imperial forces from the north and Lyranthian forces from the south.

### The [[Arcane Pulse Cannon]]

Lyranthe's forces reached Amberlight first, quickly establishing martial law. Under this occupation, Lyranthian mages and siege engineers constructed a weapon of unprecedented destructive capability—the Arcane Pulse Cannon. This extraordinary device combined ritual stones, magical foci, and precision-cut lenses capable of gathering ambient magical energy and concentrating it into a devastating energy beam. The weapon was installed atop an abandoned watchtower before the Imperial forces could arrive.

### The Single Shot

The Arcane Pulse Cannon fired only once, targeting the summit of the Exalted Peak. The consequences far exceeded all expectations as the concentrated magical energy shattered the mountain upon impact. The resulting explosion triggered massive tremors felt throughout Cylostra, sending enormous debris and molten material primarily onto the advancing Imperial Army.
The magical discharge not only consumed the ambient arcane energy but drained the life force from the entire city of Amberlight, leaving it completely devoid of life. The weapon itself collapsed after firing, crashing through the city below and causing further destruction.

> "The sky turned white, then black, then a colour for which we have no name and was every colour as well. When our vision returned, the mountain was no more, and neither was Amberlight. All that remained was silence, ash, and the beginning of a terrible understanding." 
> 
> — From the journal of a Kaskond army survivor

## Aftermath and Consequences

The catastrophic destruction inflicted by the Battle of the Thousand effectively ended active hostilities. Both sides, severely weakened and horrified by the scale of devastation, retreated into a cold stalemate that persists to the present day.


### Founding of Geldeon

A small number of Amberlight survivors escaped the ruined city, traveling northward to establish a new settlement atop the flattened peak of the once, towering mountain. This plateau, now surrounded by three lower peaks collectively known as the Exalted Peaks, became the foundation for [[Geldeon|Geldeon]].
These Amberlit survivors, who would later become known as the Prenyan Houses, established Geldeon as a new power centre that would eventually grow into the wealthiest city in post, war Cylostra.


## Legacy

The Rending permanently altered the political landscape of Cylostra, fracturing the once, unified empire into three distinct power centers: Geldeon: The Jewel of Cylostra, The Frozen City of Lyranthe, and Kaskond: The Waining Goliath. The destruction of Amberlight and the creation of the wasteland surrounding the former Exalted Peak stand as enduring reminders of the war's devastation.
Thirty years after the conclusion of hostilities, the psychological scars of the conflict continue to influence inter, city relations, with the cold peace maintained more through mutual exhaustion and fear of another catastrophe because for all we know, every amber soul is innocent.
