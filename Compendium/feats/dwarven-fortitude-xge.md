# Dwarven Fortitude
*Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 74*  

**Prerequisite**: [Dwarf](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/races/dwarf.md)

You have the blood of dwarf heroes flowing through your veins. You gain the following benefits:

- Increase your Constitution score by 1, to a maximum of 20.  
- Whenever you take the [Dodge](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/actions.md#Dodge) action in combat, you can spend one Hit Die to heal yourself. Roll the die, add your Constitution modifier, and regain a number of hit points equal to the total (minimum of 1).
