# Keoghtom's Ointment
*Wondrous item, minor, uncommon*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/keoghtoms-ointment.webp#right)


This glass jar, 3 inches in diameter, contains `1d4 + 1` doses of a thick mixture that smells faintly of aloe. The jar and its contents weigh ½ pound.

As an action, one dose of the ointment can be swallowed or applied to the skin. The creature that receives it regains `2d8 + 2` hit points, ceases to be [poisoned](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned), and is cured of any disease.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 179. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span> (as "Restorative Ointment") and the Basic Rules (2014)*
