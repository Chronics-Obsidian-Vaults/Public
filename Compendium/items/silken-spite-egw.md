# Silken Spite
*Weapon ([rapier](Compendium/items/rapier.md)), artifact (requires attunement)*  


- **Damage**: 1d8 piercing
- **Properties**: [Finesse](Rules/item-properties.md#Finesse)
- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

The blade of this rapier is made of spider silk. Its onyx pommel is tipped with a ruby cut in the shape of a spider, and the blade carries the blessing of Lolth.
