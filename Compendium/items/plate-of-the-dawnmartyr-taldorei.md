# Plate of the Dawnmartyr
*Heavy armor ([plate armor](Compendium/items/plate-armor.md)), legendary (requires attunement)*  


- **Armor Class**: 18
- **Strength**: Requires 15 STR.
- **Stealth**: The wearer has disadvantage on Stealth (DEX) checks.
- **Weight**: 65.0 lbs.

Beautiful silver scrolling and priceless, cut rubies adorn this set of brass-and-gold armor. Worn by the High Priest Duana of the Dawnfather as she fell in the Battle of Ghor Dranas.

You gain a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor. When you are struck by a melee attack, you may use your reaction to cause the attacker to suffer `1d6` points of fire damage.

## Awakened

When a character [awakens](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Plate of the Dawnmartyr, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to AC to +2.  
- You gain immunity to the [frightened](Rules/conditions.md#Frightened) condition.  
- You gain resistance to fire damage.  
- The damage inflicted on an attacker by the armor increases to `2d6` fire damage.  

## Exalted

When a character [exalts](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Plate of the Dawnmartyr, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to AC to +3.  
- If you are knocked [unconscious](Rules/conditions.md#Unconscious) by an attack or spell, a blast of healing flame surrounds you. You immediately regain 1 hit point and are lifted to your feet. All enemy creatures within 10 feet of you suffer `2d6` fire damage. You cannot use this ability again until you finish a long rest.  

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 115*
