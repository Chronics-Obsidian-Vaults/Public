# Spies' Murmur
*Wondrous item, uncommon (requires attunement)*  



This headpiece, crafted from dark metal, is worn curved around the ear. If you know a creature wearing another Spies' Murmur and that creature is within 1 mile of you, you can communicate telepathically with each other. As a bonus action, you can allow that creature to hear everything you hear for 1 hour. You can end this effect as a bonus action, and it ends if you're [incapacitated](Rules/conditions.md#Incapacitated).

*Source: Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica p. 181*
