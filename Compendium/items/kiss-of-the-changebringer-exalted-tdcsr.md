# Kiss of the Changebringer (Exalted)
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![An intricate silver necklace with emerald gems.](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/kissofthechangebringer.webp#right)

- **Properties**: [Vestige of Divergence](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Vestige%20of%20Divergence)

A beautiful, glittering emerald is set within this silver chain, humming with power and possibility. It is said that this amulet was stolen from the neck of [The Changebringer](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/deities/exandria-the-changebringer-tdcsr.md) herself by the [The Lord of the Hells](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/deities/exandria-the-lord-of-the-hells-tdcsr.md), and locked within the vaults of Bazzoxan in "Wildemount". It was later claimed there by a group of Aurora Watch soldiers during an expedition into that grim fortress's abandoned temples.

## Dormant

While the Kiss of the Changebringer is in a dormant state, you have a +1 bonus to saving throws and are immune to the [grappled](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Grappled) condition while wearing it.

## Awakened

When the Kiss of the Changebringer reaches an awakened state, you gain the following benefits:

- The bonus to saving throws increases to +2.  
- You are immune to the [restrained](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Restrained) condition.  
- You can use an action to cast the [blink](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/blink.md) spell on yourself. This property of the amulet can't be used again until you finish a long rest.  

## Exalted

When the Kiss of the Changebringer reaches an exalted state, you gain the following benefits:

- The bonus to saving throws increases to +3.  
- You are immune to the [paralyzed](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Paralyzed) and [petrified](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Petrified) conditions.  
- When you make an attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, you can roll an additional `d20`. You can choose to do so after you make the attack roll, ability check, or saving throw, but before the outcome is determined. You choose which of the d20s (`2d20`) to use for your roll. This property of the amulet can't be used again until you finish a short or long rest.  

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting Reborn p. 206*
