# Dagger of Venom
*Weapon ([dagger](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/dagger.md)), major, rare*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/dagger-of-venom.webp#right)

- **Damage**: 1d4 piercing
- **Range**: 20/60
- **Properties**: [Finesse](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Finesse), [Light](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Light), [Thrown](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Thrown)
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

You gain a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with this magic weapon.

You can use an action to cause thick, black poison to coat the blade. The poison remains for 1 minute or until an attack using this weapon hits a creature. That creature must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or take `2d10` poison damage and become [poisoned](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) for 1 minute. The dagger can't be used this way again until the next dawn.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 161. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
