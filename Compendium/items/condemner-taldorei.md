# Condemner
*Weapon ([heavy crossbow](Compendium/items/heavy-crossbow.md)), legendary (requires attunement)*  


- **Damage**: 1d10 piercing
- **Range**: 100/400
- **Properties**: [Ammunition](Rules/item-properties.md#Ammunition), [Heavy](Rules/item-properties.md#Heavy), [Loading](Rules/item-properties.md#Loading), [Two-Handed](Rules/item-properties.md#Two-Handed)
- **Weight**: 18.0 lbs.

This shadow-touched hand crossbow once served as the weapon of choice for the legendary assassin Todora. You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls made with this weapon. When you hit with an attack using this weapon, the target takes an additional `1d4` poison damage.

If you hit with an attack using this weapon, instead of dealing damage, you can choose one of two effects to trigger: A [fog cloud](Compendium/spells/fog-cloud.md) spell centered on the target, or a [silence](Compendium/spells/silence.md) spell centered on the target. You can use each effect once, and cannot use them again until you finish a short rest.

## Awakened

When a character [awakens](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Condemner, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to attack rolls to a +2.  
- Increase the poison damage inflicted on a hit to 1d6 poison damage.  
- You gain an additional effect that can be triggered in place of damage: the target can be affected by a [hold person](Compendium/spells/hold-person.md) spell (spell save DC 15).  

## Exalted

When a character [exalts](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Condemner, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to attack rolls to a +3.  
- Increase the poison damage inflicted on a hit to `1d8` poison damage.  
- The spell save DC for the [hold person](Compendium/spells/hold-person.md) spell increases to DC 17.  
- You gain an additional effect that can be triggered in place of damage: the [blight](Compendium/spells/blight.md) spell (spell save DC 17).  

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 112*
