# Black Sap
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 300 gp

This tarry substance harvested from the dark boughs of the death's head willow is a powerful intoxicant. It can be smoked as a concentrate or injected directly into the bloodstream. A creature subjected to a dose of black sap cannot be [charmed](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Charmed) or [frightened](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Frightened) for `1d6` hours.

For each dose of black sap consumed, a creature must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or become [poisoned](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) for `2d4` hours—an effect that is cumulative with multiple doses.

*Source: Explorer's Guide to Wildemount p. 152*
