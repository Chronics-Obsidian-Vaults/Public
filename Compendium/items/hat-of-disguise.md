# Hat of Disguise
*Wondrous item, major, uncommon (requires attunement)*  



While wearing this hat, you can use an action to cast the [disguise self](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/disguise-self.md) spell from it at will. The spell ends if the hat is removed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 173. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
