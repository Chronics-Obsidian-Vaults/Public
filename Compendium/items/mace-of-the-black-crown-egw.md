# Mace of the Black Crown
*Weapon ([mace](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/mace.md)), artifact (requires attunement)*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/mace-of-the-black-crown.webp#right)

- **Damage**: 1d6 bludgeoning
- **Properties**: [Vestige of Divergence](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Vestige%20of%20Divergence)
- **Weight**: 4.0 lbs.

This mace has a haft of black iron and a ruby head with a fiendish countenance. Carrying the boon of Asmodeus, it is fit for the most powerful servants of the Nine Hells.
