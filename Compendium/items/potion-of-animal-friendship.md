# Potion of Animal Friendship
*Potion, minor, uncommon*  



When you drink this potion, you can cast the [animal friendship](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/animal-friendship.md) spell (save DC 13) for 1 hour at will. Agitating this muddy liquid brings little bits into view: a fish scale, a hummingbird tongue, a cat claw, or a squirrel hair.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 187. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
