# Wand of Smiles
*Wand, minor, common*  


- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This wand has 3 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges and target a humanoid you can see within 30 feet of you. The target must succeed on a DC 10 Charisma saving throw or be forced to smile for 1 minute.

The wand regains all expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a `d20`. On a 1, the wand transforms into a [wand of scowls](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/wand-of-scowls-xge.md).

*Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 140*
