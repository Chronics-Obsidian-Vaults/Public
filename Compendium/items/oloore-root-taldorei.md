# Oloore Root
*Potion*  


- **Cost**: 100 gp

Oloore Root began as a druidic ceremonial tool for vision quests and communion with nature, but has become sought after by high society for its psychedelic properties. Drinking of the tea brewed from the root leads to heavy hallucinations, and sometimes grants insight to the world around them.

The imbiber must make a Constitution saving throw (DC 14) or become [poisoned](Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) for 1d4 hours. Upon a success, the imbiber gains advantage on any Wisdom ability checks for 1 hour. On a saving throw of 19 or 20, the imbiber gains supernatural distance seeing, as though they had cast a [scrying](Compendium/spells/scrying.md) spell. They may Concentrate on this for up to 1 minute.

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 55*
