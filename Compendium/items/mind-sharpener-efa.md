# Mind Sharpener
*Ring, uncommon (requires attunement)*  



The item has 4 charges. When you fail a Constitution saving throw to maintain Concentration, you can take a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Reaction</span> and expend 1 of the item's charges to succeed instead. The item regains `1d4` expended charges daily at dawn.

*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 112*
