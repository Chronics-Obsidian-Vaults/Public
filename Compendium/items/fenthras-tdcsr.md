# Fenthras
*Weapon ([longbow](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/longbow.md)), legendary (requires attunement)*  
![A longbow made of wood and...](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/fenthras.webp#right "A longbow made of wood and dark green vines wrapping around it, with intricate elvish designs.")

- **Damage**: 1d8 piercing
- **Range**: 150/600
- **Properties**: [Ammunition](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Ammunition), [Heavy](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Heavy), [Two-Handed](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Two-Handed), [Vestige of Divergence](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Vestige%20of%20Divergence)

This gorgeous [longbow](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/longbow.md) made of everbark from the Fey Realm flexes and shifts like a living entity. It is said to have been wielded by the legendary ranger [Vex'ahlia](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/vexahlia-tdcsr.md) against great evils.
