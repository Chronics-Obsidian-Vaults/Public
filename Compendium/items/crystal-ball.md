# Crystal Ball
*Wondrous item, major, very rare (requires attunement)*  


- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

This crystal ball is about 6 inches in diameter. While touching it, you can cast the [scrying](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/scrying.md) spell (save DC 17) with it.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 159. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
