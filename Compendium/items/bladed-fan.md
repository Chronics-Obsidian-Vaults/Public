# Bladed Fan
*Weapon (martial, monk)*  

A folding hand fan with a metal frame and honed, metal-tipped ribs. Closed it
reads as an ornament; opened and swept it opens flesh. Statistically it mirrors a
dagger, and it counts as a **monk weapon**.

- **Damage**: 1d4 piercing
- **Properties**: [Finesse](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Finesse), [Light](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Light)
- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

*Homebrew — [[Kiara]]'s signature weapon. Martial monk weapon; usable with Martial Arts.*
