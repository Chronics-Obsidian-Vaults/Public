# Malice
*Adventuring gear, poison (inhaled)*  


- **Properties**: [Poison](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Poison)
- **Cost**: 250 gp

A creature subjected to this poison must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or become [poisoned](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) for 1 hour. The [poisoned](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned) creature is [blinded](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Blinded).

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 258. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
