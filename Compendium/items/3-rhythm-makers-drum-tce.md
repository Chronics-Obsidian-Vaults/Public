# +3 Rhythm-Maker's Drum
*Wondrous item, instrument, very rare (requires attunement by a bard)*  


- **Weight**: 3.0 lbs.

While holding this [drum](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/drum.md), you gain a +3 bonus to spell attack rolls and to the saving throw DCs of your bard spells.

As an action, you can play the [drum](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/drum.md) to regain one use of your Bardic Inspiration feature. This property of the [drum](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/drum.md) can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: Tasha's Cauldron of Everything p. 134*
