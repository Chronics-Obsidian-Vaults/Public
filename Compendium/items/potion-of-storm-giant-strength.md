# Potion of Storm Giant Strength
*Potion, minor, legendary*  



When you drink this potion, your Strength score changes to 29 for 1 hour. The potion has no effect on you if your Strength is equal to or greater than that score.

This potion's transparent liquid has floating in it a sliver of fingernail from a [storm giant](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/giant/storm-giant.md).

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 187. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
