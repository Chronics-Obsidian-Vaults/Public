# Honor's Last Stand
*Armor ([shield](Compendium/items/shield.md)), legendary (requires attunement by a non-evil character)*  


- **Armor Class**: 2
- **Weight**: 6.0 lbs.

This massive, silvered shield bearing the crest of the Platinum Dragon never tarnishes. The metal is warm to the touch. While holding the shield, you have a +1 bonus to AC. This bonus is in addition to the shield's normal bonus to AC. You are also immune to the [prone](Rules/conditions.md#Prone) condition while bearing this shield.

## Awakened

When a character [awakens](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Honor's Last Stand, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to AC to +2.  
- Whenever a creature's attack against you misses, you can use your reaction to immediately attempt to make a shove attack against that creature.  

## Exalted

When a character [exalts](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Honor's Last Stand, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to AC to +3.  
- Using a bonus action, you can protect an adjacent ally, imparting +3 to their AC until the beginning of your next turn, or they are no longer adjacent to you (whichever comes first).  

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 114*
