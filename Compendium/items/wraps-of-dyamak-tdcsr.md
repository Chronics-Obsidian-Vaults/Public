# Wraps of Dyamak
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement by a monk)*  
![Thick blood-stained cloth ...](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/wrapsofdyamak.webp#right "Thick blood-stained cloth bandages affixed to two forearms. The wraps emit a red glow.")

- **Properties**: [Vestige of Divergence](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/item-properties.md#Vestige%20of%20Divergence)

Created and wielded by the ancient warrior-monk Dyamak, these thick strips of enchanted cloth are stained to appear dark crimson with blood of unknown origin. The current resting place of the wraps is unknown.
