# Ruin Card
*Wondrous item, legendary*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/books/the-deck-of-many-things-card-reference-guide/img/16-ruin.webp#right)


As an action, you can brandish this card and use it to cast the [Disintegrate](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/disintegrate.md) spell (save DC 17). Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 15*
