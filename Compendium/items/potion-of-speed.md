# Potion of Speed
*Potion, minor, very rare*  



When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [haste](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/haste.md) spell for 1 minute (no [concentration](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Concentration) required). The potion's yellow fluid is streaked with black and swirls on its own.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 188. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
