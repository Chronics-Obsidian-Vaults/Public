# Talisman of the Sphere
*Wondrous item, major, legendary (requires attunement)*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/talisman-of-the-sphere.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

When you make an Intelligence ([Arcana](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/skills.md#Arcana)) check to control a [sphere of annihilation](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/sphere-of-annihilation.md) while you are holding this talisman, you double your proficiency bonus on the check. In addition, when you start your turn with control over a [sphere of annihilation](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/sphere-of-annihilation.md), you can use an action to levitate it 10 feet plus a number of additional feet equal to 10 × your Intelligence modifier.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 207. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
