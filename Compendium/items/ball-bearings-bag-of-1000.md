# Ball Bearings (bag of 1,000)
*Adventuring gear*  


- **Cost**: 1 gp
- **Weight**: 2.0 lbs.

As an action, you can spill these tiny metal balls from their pouch to cover a level area 10 feet square. A creature moving across the covered area must succeed on a DC 10 Dexterity saving throw or fall [prone](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Prone). A creature moving through the area at half speed doesn't need to make the saving throw.

*Source: Player's Handbook p. 151. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span> and the Basic Rules (2014)*
