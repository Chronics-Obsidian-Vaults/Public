# Wraps of Dyamak
*Wondrous item, legendary (requires attunement by a monk)*  



Created and wielded by the ancient warrior called Dyamak, these thick cloth strips of enchanted material appear to be stained with blood of unknown origin. You have a +1 bonus to attack and damage rolls made with your unarmed strikes.

Whenever you finish a short or long rest, you gain a number of temporary hit points equal to your full ki points.

## Awakened

When a character [awakens](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Wraps of Dyamak, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase your bonus to attack and damage rolls to +2.  
- As a bonus action, you can cast the spell [misty step](Compendium/spells/misty-step.md). You cannot use this ability again until you score a critical hit with an unarmed strike, or you complete a short or long rest.  

## Exalted

When a character [exalts](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Wraps of Dyamak, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase your bonus to attack and damage rolls to +3.  
- When you hit with an unarmed strike, you can choose to make the attack a Ravenous Strike. The target takes an additional `6d6` necrotic damage, and you regain hit points equal to the additional necrotic damage dealt. You cannot use this ability again until you complete a short or long rest.  

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 116*
