# Wand of Polymorph
*Wand, major, very rare (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/wand-of-polymorph.webp#right)

- **Weight**: 1.0 lbs.

This wand has 7 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 of its charges to cast the [polymorph](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/polymorph.md) spell (save DC 15) from it.

The wand regains `1d6 + 1` expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a `d20`. On a 1, the wand crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 211. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
