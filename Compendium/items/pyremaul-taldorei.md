# Pyremaul
*Weapon ([maul](Compendium/items/maul.md)), legendary (requires attunement)*  


- **Damage**: 2d6 bludgeoning
- **Properties**: [Heavy](Rules/item-properties.md#Heavy), [Two-Handed](Rules/item-properties.md#Two-Handed)
- **Weight**: 10.0 lbs.

Forged from deep red iron, this massive hammer houses an ever-burning gemstone of orange flame. You have a +1 bonus to attack rolls made with this weapon. When you hit with an attack using this weapon, the target takes an additional `1d6` fire damage.

You can choose to have this weapon shed bright light in a 30 foot radius and dim light for an additional 30 feet. If you kill a creature with an attack using this weapon, the corpse is immolated, turning to ash.

## Awakened

When a character [awakens](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Pyremaul, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to attack rolls to a +2.  
- Increase the additional fire damage inflicted by a hit to `2d6`.  
- As an action, you can slam the Pyremaul into the ground to cast the [burning hands](Compendium/spells/burning-hands.md) spell at 2nd level (spell save DC 15). You cannot use this ability again until you finish a short or long rest.  
- When you score a critical hit with this weapon, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be knocked [prone](Rules/conditions.md#Prone).  

## Exalted

When a character [exalts](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Pyremaul, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to attack and damage rolls to +3.  
- Increase the additional fire damage inflicted by a hit to `3d6`.  
- Your [burning hands](Compendium/spells/burning-hands.md) ability is now cast at 3rd level, and the spell save DC increases to 17.  
- When you score a critical hit with this weapon, the target must succeed on a DC 17 Strength saving throw or be knocked [prone](Rules/conditions.md#Prone). You can also roll an additional `3d6` fire damage when determining the extra damage for the critical hit.  

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 115*
