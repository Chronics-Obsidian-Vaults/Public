# Universal Solvent
*Wondrous item, minor, legendary*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/universal-solvent.webp#right)


This tube holds milky liquid with a strong alcohol smell. You can use an action to pour the contents of the tube onto a surface within reach. The liquid instantly dissolves up to 1 square foot of adhesive it touches, including [sovereign glue](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/sovereign-glue.md).

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 209. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
