# Key Card
*Wondrous item, legendary*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/02-key.webp#right)


As an action, you can brandish this card and use it to cast the [Knock](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/knock.md) spell, with no sound made by the spell. This property can be used five times, and it regains all expended uses at dawn.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 14*
