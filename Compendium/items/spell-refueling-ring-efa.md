# Spell-Refueling Ring
*Ring, uncommon (requires attunement by a spellcaster)*  



While wearing this ring, you can recover one expended spell slot as a <span title="Player's Handbook (2024)">Bonus Action</span>. The recovered slot can be of level 3 or lower. Once used, the ring can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: Eberron: Forge of the Artificer p. 112*
