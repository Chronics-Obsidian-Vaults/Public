# Pot of Awakening
*Wondrous item, minor, common*  



If you plant an ordinary shrub in this 10-pound clay pot and let it grow for 30 days, the shrub magically transforms into an [awakened shrub](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/plant/awakened-shrub.md) at the end of that time. When the shrub awakens, its roots break the pot, destroying it.

The awakened shrub is friendly toward you. Absent commands from you, it does nothing.

*Source: Xanathar's Guide to Everything p. 138*
