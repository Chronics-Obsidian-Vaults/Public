# Knight Card
*Wondrous item, legendary*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/09-knight.webp#right)


As an action, you can throw this card to an unoccupied space on the ground within 60 feet of yourself while speaking a command word, whereupon the card magically transforms into a [deck defender](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/construct/deck-defender-bmt.md) (see "chapter 9" for its stat block) for up to 1 minute or until you use an action to speak the command word again, whereupon it transforms back into a card. The deck defender obeys your spoken commands. If you issue no commands, it takes the [Dodge](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/actions.md#Dodge) action and moves to avoid danger. The deck defender reverts to a card early if it drops to 0 hit points. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 14*
