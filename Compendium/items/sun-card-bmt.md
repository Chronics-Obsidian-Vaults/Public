# Sun Card
*Wondrous item, legendary*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/10-sun.webp#right)


As an action, you can brandish this card and use it to cast the [Sunbeam](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/sunbeam.md) spell (save DC 17), projecting the light from the card's face. Once this property is used, it can't be used again until the next dawn.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 15*
