# Armor of the Valiant Soul
*Medium armor ([scale mail](Compendium/items/scale-mail.md)), legendary (requires attunement)*  


- **Armor Class**: 14 + Dex modifier (max of +2)
- **Stealth**: The wearer has disadvantage on Stealth (DEX) checks.
- **Weight**: 45.0 lbs.

This antique armor forged from jade and black dragon scales was once owned and worn by J'mon Sa Ord of Ank'Harel. You have a +1 bonus to AC while wearing this armor.

You can cast the [command](Compendium/spells/command.md) spell (save DC 13) while attuned to this armor. You cannot use this ability again until you have completed a long rest.

## Awakened

When a character [awakens](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Armor of the Valiant Soul, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to AC to +2.  
- You gain resistance to acid damage while wearing this armor.  
- You gain immunity to the [frightened](Rules/conditions.md#Frightened) condition while wearing this armor.  

## Exalted

When a character [exalts](Rules/variant-rules/the-advancement-of-a-vestige-taldorei.md) Armor of the Valiant Soul, apply the following changes to the item's traits:

- Increase the bonus to AC to +3.  
- You gain immunity to acid damage while you are wearing this armor.  
- Your immunity to the [frightened](Rules/conditions.md#Frightened) condition extends to include all friendly creatures within 15 feet, as long as you are conscious.  
- The spell save DC of the armor's [command](Compendium/spells/command.md) spell becomes DC 17, and it can target four additional creatures.  

*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 111*
