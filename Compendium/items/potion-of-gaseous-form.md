# Potion of Gaseous Form
*Potion, minor, rare*  



When you drink this potion, you gain the effect of the [gaseous form](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/gaseous-form.md) spell for 1 hour (no [concentration](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Concentration) required) or until you end the effect as a bonus action. This potion's container seems to hold fog that moves and pours like water.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 187. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
