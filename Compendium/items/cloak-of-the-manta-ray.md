# Cloak of the Manta Ray
*Wondrous item, minor, uncommon*  
![](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/items/img/cloak-of-the-manta-ray.webp#right)


While wearing this cloak with its hood up, you can breathe underwater, and you have a swimming speed of 60 feet. Pulling the hood up or down requires an action.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 159. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*
