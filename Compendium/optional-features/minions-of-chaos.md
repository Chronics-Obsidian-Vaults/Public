# Minions of Chaos
*Source: Player's Handbook p. 111. Available in the <span title='Systems Reference Document (5.1)'>SRD</span>*  

**Prerequisite**: Level 9

You can cast [conjure elemental](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/conjure-elemental.md) once using a warlock spell slot. You can't do so again until you finish a long rest.
