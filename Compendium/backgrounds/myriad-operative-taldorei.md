# Myriad Operative
*Source: Tal'Dorei Campaign Setting p. 106*  


- **Skill Proficiencies.** [Deception](Rules/skills.md#Deception), plus your choice of one from among [Stealth](Rules/skills.md#Stealth) and [Sleight of Hand](Rules/skills.md#Sleight%20of%20Hand)  
- **Tool Proficiencies.** Your choice of one from [Thieves' Tools](Compendium/items/thieves-tools.md), [Forgery Kit](Compendium/items/forgery-kit.md), or [Disguise Kit](Compendium/items/disguise-kit.md)  
- **Languages.** Thieves' Cant  
- **Equipment.** A set of dark [common clothes](Compendium/items/common-clothes.md) including a hood, a set of tools to match your choice of tool proficiency, and a belt [pouch](Compendium/items/pouch.md) containing 10 gp  


## Feature: A Favor In Turn

You have gained enough clout within the Myriad that you can call in a favor from your contacts, should you be close enough to a center of Myriad activity. A request for a favor can be no longer than 20 words, and is passed up the chain to an undisclosed Spireling for approval. This favor can take a shape up to the DM's discretion depending on the request, with varying speeds of fulfillment: If muscle is requested, an NPC Myriad minion can temporarily aid the party. If money is needed, a small loan can be provided. If you've been imprisoned, they can look into breaking you free, or paying off the jailer.

The turn comes when a favor is asked to be repaid. The favor debt can be called in without warning, and many times with intended immediacy. Perhaps the player is called to commit a specific burglary without the option to decline. Maybe they must press a dignitary for a specific secret at an upcoming ball. The Myriad could even request a hit on an NPC, no questions asked or answered. The DM is to provide a debt call proportionate to the favor fulfilled. If a favor is not repaid within a reasonable period of time, membership to the Myriad can be revoked, and if the debt is large enough, the player may become the next hit contract to make the rounds.

While the skill sets are similar in many ways, working with the Myriad brings some different benefits and challenges. The Myriad specializes in slavery and exotic trade, so you have access to servants and rare materials at times without using your A Favor In Turn ability. However, your access to contacts is far more limited, and should you be found out as Myriad by a member of the Clasp, you will quickly find yourself hunted. Note: This feature is a variant of the [Clasp Member](Compendium/backgrounds/clasp-member-taldorei.md) feature.

## Suggested Characteristics

Use the tables for the [Criminal](Compendium/backgrounds/criminal.md) background in the PHB as the basis for your traits and motivations, modifying the entries when appropriate to suit your identity as a member of the Myriad. Your bond is likely associated with your fellow Myriad members or the individual who introduced you to the organization. Your ideal probably involves establishing your importance and indispensability within the Myriad. You joined to improve your livelihood and sense of purpose.
