# Izzet Charm
*Charm*  

When you activate this charm, you can cast the [dispel magic](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/dispel-magic.md) or [lightning bolt](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/lightning-bolt.md) spell. Alternatively, as an action, you can regain one expended spell slot of 3rd level or lower. The charm vanishes after you activate it.

*Source: Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica p. 174*
