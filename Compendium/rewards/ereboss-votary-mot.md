# Erebos's Votary
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 10+ Erebos trait*

You can cast [vampiric touch](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/vampiric-touch.md) with this trait. Once you cast the spell in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 43*
