# Charm of the Sage

You can cast [Divination](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/divination.md), requiring no material components and using your Intelligence as the spellcasting ability. Once you cast the spell three times, the charm vanishes from you.

*Source: The Book of Many Things p. 62*
