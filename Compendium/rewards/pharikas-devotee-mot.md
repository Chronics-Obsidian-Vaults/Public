# Pharika's Devotee
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 3+ Pharika trait*

As a devotee of Pharika, you have power over affliction. You can cast [ray of sickness](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/ray-of-sickness.md) with this trait a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 70*
