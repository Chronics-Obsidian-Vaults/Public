# Boon of the Fire Soul

You have immunity to fire damage. You can also cast [burning hands](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/burning-hands.md) (save DC 15) at will, without using a spell slot or any components. 

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 232*
