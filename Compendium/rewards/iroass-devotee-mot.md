# Iroas's Devotee
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 3+ Iroas trait*

As a devotee of Iroas, you have earned his favor through victories won in his name. You can cast [compelled duel](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/compelled-duel.md) with this trait a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. Charisma is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 49*
