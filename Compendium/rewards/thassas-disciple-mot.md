# Thassa's Disciple
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 25+ Thassa trait*

You are inspired by the tempestuous, uncontrollable nature of the sea; you have advantage on saving throws against being [charmed](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Charmed) or [restrained](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Restrained).
