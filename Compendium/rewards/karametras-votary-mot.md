# Karametra's Votary
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 10+ Karametra trait*

You can cast [create food and water](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/create-food-and-water.md) with this trait. Once you cast the spell in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

In addition, you have advantage on saving throws against being [poisoned](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Poisoned).

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 52*
