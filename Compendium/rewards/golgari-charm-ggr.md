# Golgari Charm
*Charm*  

When you activate this charm, you can cast the [dispel magic](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/dispel-magic.md), [false life](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/false-life.md) (3rd-level version), or [stinking cloud](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/stinking-cloud.md) spell. The charm vanishes after you activate it.

*Source: Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica p. 173*
