# Boon of Undetectability

You gain a +10 bonus to Dexterity ([Stealth](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/skills.md#Stealth)) checks, and you can't be detected or targeted by divination magic, including scrying sensors.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 232*
