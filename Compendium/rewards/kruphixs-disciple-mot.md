# Kruphix's Disciple
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 25+ Kruphix trait*

As a true champion of Kruphix, you can use your [mage hand](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/mage-hand.md) cantrip to protect yourself. While your spectral hand is within 5 feet of you, you gain a +2 bonus to your AC against ranged attacks.

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 61*
