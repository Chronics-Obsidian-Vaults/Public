# Rakdos Charm
*Charm*  

When you activate this charm, you can cast the [crown of madness](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/crown-of-madness.md), [hellish rebuke](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/hellish-rebuke.md), or [shatter](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/shatter.md) spell. The charm vanishes after you activate it.

*Source: Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica p. 174*
