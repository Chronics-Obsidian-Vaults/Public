# Charm of Darkvision

This charm allows you to cast the [darkvision](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/darkvision.md) spell as an action, no components required. Once used three times, the charm goes away.

*Source: Dungeon Master's Guide p. 228*
