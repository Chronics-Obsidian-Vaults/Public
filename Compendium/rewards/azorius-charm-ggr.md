# Azorius Charm
*Charm*  

When you activate this charm, you can cast the [hold person](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/hold-person.md) (3rd-level version), [command](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/command.md), or [counterspell](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/counterspell.md) spell. The charm vanishes after you activate it.

*Source: Guildmasters' Guide to Ravnica p. 173*
