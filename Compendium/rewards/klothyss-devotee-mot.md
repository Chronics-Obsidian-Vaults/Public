# Klothys's Devotee
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 3+ Klothys trait*

As a devotee of Klothys, you can manipulate the bonds of destiny that invisibly entangle every living thing. You can cast [command](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/command.md) with this trait a number of times equal to your Wisdom modifier (minimum of once). You regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 58*
