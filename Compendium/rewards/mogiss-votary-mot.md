# Mogis's Votary
*Piety Trait*  

*Piety 10+ Mogis trait*

You can cast [blinding smite](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/spells/blinding-smite.md) with this trait. Once you cast the spell in this way, you can't do so again until you finish a long rest. Constitution is your spellcasting ability for this spell.

*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 64*
