# Canyon Shrine Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 154* 

**Canyon Shrine Villains**

| dice: d8 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | A [minotaur](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/monstrosity/minotaur.md) captures and devours other creatures limb by limb as part of a bizarre ritual honoring Mogis. |
| 2 | A tribe of [berserkers](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/berserker.md) ride [giant bats](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/beast/giant-bat.md) and hunt humanoids that travel through the canyon. |
| 3 | An [adult blue dragon](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/dragon/adult-blue-dragon.md) enslaves the inhabitants of the caves, intent on building an army to conquer a neighboring settlement. |
| 4 | A [manticore](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/monstrosity/manticore.md) captures warriors and forces them to fight to the death for its amusement. |
| 5 | A group of [cyclopes](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/giant/cyclops.md) capture travelers and keep them as livestock in their caves. |
| 6 | A [lamia](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/monstrosity/lamia.md) lures young nobles into its cult with promises of hedonism and sells those who disappoint it to other canyon inhabitants. |
| 7 | A vicious [gynosphinx](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/monstrosity/gynosphinx.md) captures groups of people, then forces one person to choose which of the others should be devoured first. |
| 8 | The victims of the canyon's inhabitants rise as [wraiths](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/undead/wraith.md) determined to end all life in the area. |
