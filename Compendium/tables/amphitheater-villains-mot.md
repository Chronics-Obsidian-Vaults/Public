# Amphitheater Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 166* 

**Amphitheater Villains**

| dice: d8 | Villain |
|----------|---------|
| 1 | An actor charms nobles who see him perform into surrendering their fortunes. |
| 2 | The [wraith](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/undead/wraith.md) of a murdered actress reenacts her death every night, playing the role of the murderer and targeting a new performer in her place. |
| 3 | An untalented dancer ([scout](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/scout.md)) poisons those with more skill in order to get ahead. |
| 4 | A [mage](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/mage.md) playwright invites political figures to see his satires, then uses magic to make them react dramatically, which encourages more people to see his plays. |
| 5 | A leading lady is also an [assassin](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/assassin.md) for hire, and she uses a secret room in the theater as her base. |
| 6 | Burglars ([veterans](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/veteran.md)) masquerading as acrobats identify marks during their performances, then rob them afterward. |
| 7 | A murderer ([spy](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/spy.md)) who works as a prop maker hides his weapons and victims in among pieces of the set. |
| 8 | A theater-loving [mage](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/mage.md) uses a [homunculus](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/construct/homunculus.md) to spy on audience members to gain blackmail material. |
