# Graveyard Temple Villains
*Source: Mythic Odysseys of Theros p. 126* 

**Graveyard Temple Villains**

| dice: d6 | Villain |

| 5 | An [archmage](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/archmage.md) works to raise a vengeful army from the remains of her war-ravaged village. |
| 6 | A mob of paranoid, inexperienced [commoners](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Compendium/bestiary/humanoid/commoner.md) hunt for Returned but end up tormenting all strangers. |
