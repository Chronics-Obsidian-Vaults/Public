# Grease
*1st-level, Conjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Components:** V, S, M (a bit of pork rind or butter)
- **Duration:** 1 minute

Slick grease covers the ground in a 10-foot square centered on a point within range and turns it into difficult terrain for the duration.

When the grease appears, each creature standing in its area must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall [prone](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Prone). A creature that enters the area or ends its turn there must also succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall [prone](/Campaigns/Chronics%20of%20the%20Times%20Before/Public/Rules/conditions.md#Prone).
