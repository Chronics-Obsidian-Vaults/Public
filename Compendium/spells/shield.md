# Shield
*1st-level, Abjuration*  


- **Casting time:** 1 Reaction, which you take when you are hit by an attack or targeted by the [magic missile](Compendium/spells/magic-missile.md) spell
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V, S
- **Duration:** 1 round

An invisible barrier of magical force appears and protects you. Until the start of your next turn, you have a +5 bonus to AC, including against the triggering attack, and you take no damage from [magic missile](Compendium/spells/magic-missile.md).

**Classes**: [Bard (Magical Secrets)](Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-bard.md); [Fighter (Eldritch Knight)](Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-eldritch-knight.md); [Rogue (Arcane Trickster)](Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-arcane-trickster.md); [Sorcerer](Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-sorcerer.md); [Warlock (The Hexblade)](Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-the-hexblade-xge.md "subclass=XGE"); [Wizard](Compendium/lists/list-spells-classes-wizard.md)
