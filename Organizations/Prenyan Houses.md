# The Prenyan Houses

## Overview

The Prenyan Houses represent the elite familial dynasties of [[Geldeon|Geldeon]], occupying the most coveted real estate at the summit of the city's mountain slopes. Their concentrated wealth and influence form the backbone of Geldeon's economic and political landscape within the city's largely autonomous borders. These ancient bloodlines trace their origins to the survivors of Amberlight who founded Geldeon atop the flattened peak of the former Exalted Peak, and they have adapted to thrive in the post-war isolation that defines modern Geldeon.

Although there is no formal name for the houses, they can all be traced to one [[Nadyha Prenya]] as she is accredited with leading the migration efforts to the peak of the devastated mountain.

## The Three Upper Districts

The Prenyan Houses are organized geographically into three distinct districts, each with its own character and specializations suited to Geldeon's self-sufficient economy:

### The Prenya District

Named after the founding house, this central district serves as the political and cultural heart of the upper city. The district's elevated position provides commanding views in all directions and houses the most politically influential families.

#### House Prenya
The founding house and traditional leaders among the Prenyan dynasties. House Prenya has built their vast wealth through:
* Extensive patronage and financial backing of artisan guilds throughout the city
* Private security services for wealthy individuals and businesses who can afford protection
* Discreet banking services within Geldeon's closed economy
* Intelligence gathering within Geldeon's districts

Their support of the artisan guilds has made them indispensable to Geldeon's self-sufficient economy, while their private security forces serve as elite protection for those who distrust the Quorum's enforcers.

#### House Valdris
Masters of information and internal communication networks:
* Courier and messaging services throughout the city's vertical layout
* Newspaper and press circulation
* Record keeping and administrative services
* Cultural preservation and maintenance of pre-war Amberlight traditions

#### House Stormwind
Masters of resource management and environmental control:
* Weather monitoring and prediction for mining and agriculture
* Management of the city's water resources and distribution
* Agricultural oversight for Geldeon's limited food production
* Natural resource conservation and sustainability planning

### The Akveta District

Located on the eastern slopes, this district focuses on luxury goods production and the limited high-value trade that Geldeon permits.

#### House Zothello
Renowned for their mastery of luxury goods and selective trade operations:
* Careful management of Geldeon's rare external trade relationships
* Cultural refinement and social status cultivation
* Exclusive access to luxury materials through discrete channels

#### House Silvermere
Controllers of precious metal working and financial services:
* Advanced metallurgy and fine jewelry crafting
* Currency exchange and precious metal trading within the city
* Banking services for the upper classes
* Investment management for wealthy families

#### House Korrath
Masters of internal logistics and distribution:
* Coordination of goods movement throughout Geldeon's vertical districts
* Warehouse management and storage solutions
* Supply chain optimization within the closed economy
* Transportation services between the city's elevation levels

### The Gruhor District

Positioned on the western slopes, this district commands mining operations and serves as the industrial backbone of Geldeon's economy.

#### House Gruhor
A dwarf family that dominates Geldeon's mining operations:
* Extensive mining claims throughout the [[Exalted Peaks]]
* Specialized knowledge of underground extraction and tunneling
* Management of the city's primary source of raw materials
* Traditional dwarven craftsmanship in stonework and metalworking

Their dwarven heritage and generational expertise in mining have made them absolutely essential to Geldeon's self-sufficiency, providing the raw materials that fuel the city's internal economy.

#### House Ironhold
Specialists in construction and architectural engineering:
* Mountain construction techniques and structural engineering
* Maintenance of Geldeon's complex vertical infrastructure
* Quarrying operations and stone preparation
* Civil engineering projects throughout the city

#### House Thornwick
Specialists in magical research and enchantment services:
* Magical enhancement of everyday items for local use
* Protective ward systems for wealthy estates
* Arcane consultation for guild operations
* Maintenance of the city's magical infrastructure

## Political Influence

The relationship between the Prenyan Houses and [[Tolay Quorum|The Tolay Quorum]] represents the most important political dynamic in Geldeon. In the city's autonomous, post-war environment, internal politics have become even more critical as external diplomatic options remain limited.

It is widely suspected that a majority of Quorum members are drawn from Prenyan bloodlines or their direct representatives. Each district traditionally provides five members to the Quorum, with houses rotating influence based on complex political negotiations and the city's economic needs.

The houses maintain elaborate networks of informants attempting to discover Quorum identities, while the Quorum's enforcers ensure that even the wealthiest families remain subject to the council's authority. This balance of power prevents any single house from dominating Geldeon's governance while maintaining the secrecy that protects the city's leadership.

## Economic Structure in Isolation

The Prenyan Houses have adapted their economic strategies to Geldeon's largely autonomous status following [[The Cylostran Civil War|the Rending]]. With foreign trade severely limited and tensions remaining high with other regions, the houses focus on:

* Internal resource circulation and value creation
* Maximizing efficiency within Geldeon's closed economy
* Supporting the guild system that maintains the city's self-sufficiency
* Carefully managing the few external trade relationships that exist

Each house maintains exclusive control over certain critical economic functions while forming necessary alliances to ensure the city's continued prosperity despite its isolation.

## Relationship with Lower Districts

The Prenyan Houses maintain complex relationships with Geldeon's lower districts:

* **Sorento**: The upper-middle and middle-class residential district serves as a buffer zone, with some Prenyan influence through property ownership and service provision
* **Martslock**: The guild and manufacturing district is heavily supported by House Prenya's patronage and other houses' resource provision
* **Holbeck**: The lowest district in both altitude and wealth receives minimal direct Prenyan attention, though their labor is essential to the city's function
* **Batpol**: The entertainment district under Geldeon's jurisdiction where wealthy individuals indulge, providing significant revenue to several houses

## Cultural Impact

The Prenyan Houses shape Geldeon's cultural landscape through their district-specific contributions, adapted to the city's autonomous status:

The Prenya District establishes intellectual and artistic trends while preserving Amberlight's cultural heritage. The Akveta District influences luxury consumption and refined social customs. The Gruhor District maintains practical traditions and work ethics essential to the city's self-sufficiency.

## Current Adaptations

In the three decades since the Rending ended, the Prenyan Houses have fundamentally restructured their operations:

The houses have abandoned external expansion in favor of maximizing internal efficiency and control. They focus on technological innovation and process improvement within Geldeon's borders, while carefully managing the psychological challenges of isolation through cultural and entertainment investments.

Their success in maintaining Geldeon's prosperity despite its autonomous status has proven that the city can thrive independently, making the Prenyan Houses the de facto guardians of this new model of urban governance.
