# The Tolay Quorum

## Overview

The governance of [[Geldeon|Geldeon]], the wealthiest city in [[Cylostra|Cylostra]], is entrusted to an anonymous council known as the Tolay Quorum. This secretive body consists of 15 carefully selected individuals who operate largely from the shadows, making decisions that shape the city's future without revealing their identities to the public.

### Composition and Selection

The Quorum members officially can be whoever is within Geldeon, but are usually drawn from various powerful factions within Geldeon:
* High-ranking court officials
* Influential guild masters
* Military leaders of distinction
* Renowned heroes of the city
* Members of the [[Prenyan Houses]] (suspected to form the majority)

Members are secretly elected through processes known only to those in the upper echelons of Geldeon's power structure. The turnover of membership occurs with surprising frequency, suggesting either term limits or strategic replacements to maintain the Quorum's anonymity and effectiveness.

### The Quorum's Voice

While the council itself remains hidden, a single designated individual known as "the Quorum's Voice" serves as the public face of the governing body. This person:
* Communicates all official decrees and decisions
* Represents the Quorum in public functions
* Acts as intermediary between the citizens and the secret council
* Holds no actual voting power or influence over the Quorum's decisions

### Methods and Enforcement

The Quorum operates through a combination of:
* Secret deliberations in undisclosed locations
* Encrypted communications
* Proxy representatives
* Anonymous enforcement agents

Their effectiveness relies on absolute secrecy, maintained through a lethal enforcement policy. Former members and those who discover Quorum identities face swift and untraceable elimination, creating a climate of fear that ensures discretion.

> "One face for fifteen shadows, and shadows leave no footprints in the streets of Geldeon." 
> 
> — Common saying in the lower city taverns

### Historical Context

The Quorum's establishment appears to have occurred following the devastating "Rending" civil war that tore through Cylostra. As Geldeon positioned itself as the region's dominant power, this secretive governance model likely emerged as a way to protect leadership from assassination and to make unified decisions without the divisive politics that had previously led to conflict.
