# Session 2 - The Beach Episode

**Date**: 6th June 2025 | **In-Game Date**: 32nd Neiagurth 3996

## Summary

After the devastating caravan attack, our unlikely group of survivors came together to help each other reach safety in [[Geldeon]]. The journey took us through various landmarks and eventually to the [[Saphra River]], where we took time to rest, recover, and get to know each other better through some unexpectedly lighthearted moments.

## Key Events
1. Party formed and decided to travel together to [[Geldeon]]
2. Carried the unconscious [[Hermes|soldier]] along the journey
3. [[Axel]] shared info on the caravan's origins
4. Discovered [[Aesir|The Aesir]] - a mysterious ship buried in sand
5. Reached [[Saphra River]] and had some playful downtime

## Locations Visited
