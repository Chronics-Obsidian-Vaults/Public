# Session 1 - The Caravan

**Date**: 26 May 2025 | **In-Game Date**: 30th Neiagurth 3996



## Summary

A caravan filled with various goods departed from [[Geldeon]] bound for [[Lyranthe]], but never reached its destination. The convoy was attacked by an undead sorcerer and their minions near [[Amberlight]]'s outskirts, leaving only four survivors. A mysterious elven figure appeared and helped the survivors escape the devastation.

## Key Events
1. Party met unaccounted for at the [[Artisan's Guild]] as the caravan prepared to depart
2. Travelled from [[Geldeon]] along the [[Obsidian Path]]
3. Attacked by [[Dalxicis|an undead dragonborn]] and an undead horde north of [[Amberlight]]
4. Aided by [[Kharis]], a mysterious elf

## Locations Visited
- **[[Geldeon]]**: Starting point at the [[Artisan's Guild]]
- **[[Obsidian Path]]**: A natural obsidian road - a reminder of the war
- **[[Amberlight]] Outskirts**: Rocky wasteland where the caravan was attacked
