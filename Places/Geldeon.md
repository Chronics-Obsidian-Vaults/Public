# Geldeon: The Jewel of Cylostra

## Overview

Geldeon stands as the largest and wealthiest city among the three main regions of [[Cylostra|Cylostra]], operating as a largely autonomous city-state in the post-war era. Built upon the flattened summit and slopes of what was once the towering [[Exalted Peaks|Exalted Peak]], the city's unique geography creates a dramatic multi-tiered urban landscape that rises organically from the mountain's remains. Following [[The Cylostran Civil War|the Rending]], Geldeon has embraced isolation and self-sufficiency, creating a closed but prosperous economy that demonstrates the city's resilience and organizational capabilities.

## Geographic Foundation

The city rests upon the flattened plateau created when the Exalted Peak was destroyed during the Battle of the Thousand. This circular plateau, approximately three miles in diameter, forms the foundation for Geldeon's unique tiered architecture. The surrounding slopes descend in carefully planned terraces that maximize both defensibility and efficient resource distribution throughout the vertical city.

## The Upper City Districts

Three distinct districts house [[Prenyan Houses|The Prenyan Houses]] and the city's elite, each occupying a different slope of the mountain:

### The Prenya District

Located on the southern slopes, this district serves as the political and cultural centre of the upper city. The district's terraced architecture features grand estate complexes with private gardens, cultural institutions preserving [[Amberlight|Amberlight's]] heritage, and the administrative centres where the city's autonomous governance is coordinated.

The district's elevation provides strategic oversight of the entire city while maintaining the privacy essential to Geldeon's secretive political system.

### The Akveta District

Positioned on the eastern slopes, this district focuses on luxury production and the careful management of Geldeon's limited external trade relationships. The area features specialized workshops producing high-value goods, private estates with extensive gardens, and discrete facilities for managing the city's rare but valuable foreign contacts.

The district's eastern exposure provides optimal conditions for the terraced production facilities that create the luxury goods essential to maintaining Geldeon's economic independence.

### The Gruhor District

Commanding the western slopes, this district serves as the industrial and resource extraction centre of the upper city. Dominated by the dwarven House Gruhor, the area features mining operation headquarters, resource processing facilities, and the technical infrastructure that supports Geldeon's self-sufficient economy.

The district's position allows direct oversight of the mining operations that extend deep into the mountain, providing the raw materials essential to the city's autonomous existence.

## The Middle and Lower Districts

Below the three upper districts, the city spreads across the broader mountain base and surrounding areas, organized into specialized zones that form the backbone of Geldeon's closed economy:

### Sorento

The upper-middle and middle-class residential district, Sorento occupies the middle elevations of the mountain slopes. This area features:

* Comfortable residential housing for successful guild members and minor merchants
* Local markets serving the daily needs of the middle class
* Educational institutions and community centers
* Professional services supporting the upper districts

Sorento serves as a crucial buffer zone between the Prenyan elite and the working population, housing the skilled professionals and successful artisans who keep Geldeon's autonomous economy functioning.

### Martslock

The guild and manufacturing district, Martslock represents the industrial heart of Geldeon's self-sufficient economy. Located at practical elevations for resource transport, this district contains:

* Major guild headquarters and their associated workshops
* Manufacturing facilities producing goods for internal consumption
* Coordination centers where production is planned and distributed throughout the city

The district benefits heavily from [[Prenyan Houses|House Prenya's]] patronage and serves as the primary example of how Geldeon has successfully maintained its autonomy through internal production capabilities.

### Holbeck

The lowest district in both altitude and economic status, Holbeck occupies the mountain's base and serves essential but unglamorous functions:

* Housing for laborers, miners, and service workers
* Basic manufacturing and processing facilities
* Waste management and sanitation services
* Entry-level employment opportunities for new residents

Despite its low status, Holbeck's workforce is absolutely essential to Geldeon's functioning, providing the labor that supports the entire city's autonomous economy.

### Batpol

A unique district under Geldeon's jurisdiction but physically separated from the main mountain, Batpol serves as the city's entertainment and indulgence center. This area features:

* Luxury entertainment venues and establishments
* High-end restaurants and recreational facilities
* Gambling houses and other leisure activities
* Discrete meeting places for sensitive business negotiations

Batpol provides a crucial economic function by offering wealthy residents opportunities to spend their accumulated wealth within Geldeon's closed system, preventing economic stagnation while maintaining the city's autonomous status.

## Transportation and Infrastructure

Geldeon's vertical layout and autonomous status necessitate sophisticated internal transportation systems:

### Mechanical Lifts

Arcane-powered elevator systems transport goods and people between elevation levels, with access controlled based on social status and economic necessity.

### The Stairway Networks

Extensive stone stairways provide pedestrian access throughout the city, designed with multiple routes to ensure continued function even during maintenance or security concerns.

## Autonomous Economy and Security

### Self-Sufficiency Systems

Geldeon's autonomous status requires complete internal resource management:

* Mountain springs provide water through carefully maintained distribution networks
* Terraced agriculture and controlled hunting provide food security
* Mining operations supply raw materials for all manufacturing needs
* Waste recycling and resource conservation maintain sustainability

### The Quorum's Enforcers

Rather than maintaining a traditional military, Geldeon relies on the Quorum's enforcement network:

* Elite peacekeeping forces that maintain internal order
* Specialized units for investigating threats to the autonomous system
* Border guards who carefully control access to the city
* Intelligence operatives who monitor potential external threats

These enforcers serve the [[Tolay Quorum|Tolay Quorum]] directly and operate independently of the Prenyan Houses, ensuring that even the wealthiest families remain subject to the city's governance.

## Post-War Isolation Strategy

Geldeon's geographic layout supports its autonomous strategy:

* The vertical city design makes external attack extremely difficult
* Multiple elevation levels provide redundancy for essential services
* The closed economy reduces dependence on potentially hostile neighbors
* Internal production capabilities ensure survival during extended isolation

### Limited External Relations

While largely autonomous, Geldeon maintains carefully controlled relationships:

* Minimal trade in high-value, low-volume goods that cannot be produced internally
* Intelligence gathering to monitor potential threats from [[Kaskond|Kaskond]] and [[Lyranthe|Lyranthe]]
* Discrete diplomatic contacts for crisis management
* Refugee screening and selective immigration for essential skills

> "Geldeon stands alone by choice, not by accident. Every stone in our walls, every coin in our vaults, every life in our streets—all exist because we chose to press on." 
> 
> — The Quorum's Voice, addressing the city during the annual Founder's Day ceremony

## Current Challenges and Adaptations

The city's autonomous existence presents ongoing challenges:

* Maintaining technological advancement without external knowledge exchange
* Managing population growth within limited physical and resource constraints
* Preventing economic stagnation in a closed system
* Addressing the psychological effects of isolation on the population

Despite these challenges, Geldeon's success as an autonomous city-state has proven that careful planning, internal resource management, and effective governance can create prosperity even in isolation, making it the dominant power in post-war Cylostra through strength rather than conquest.
