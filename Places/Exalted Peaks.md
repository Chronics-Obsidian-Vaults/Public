# Exalted Peaks

The Exalted Peaks are a mountain range in central [[Cylostra]], fundamentally altered during [[The Cylostran Civil War]]. Originally a single towering mountain known as the Exalted Peak, the range now consists of a flattened central plateau surrounded by three lower peaks, the product of catastrophic magical destruction during the Battle of the Thousand. The plateau serves as the foundation for [[Geldeon]], whilst the surrounding peaks remain largely wild, with extensive mining operations throughout the range.

## Geography

### Pre-war configuration

Before the Cylostran Civil War, the Exalted Peak stood as one of Cylostra's most prominent geographical features, part of an imposing mountain range whose summits could be seen from hundreds of miles away on clear days across the [[Alderian Expanse]]. The mountain's elevation and strategic position made it a dominant landmark in the region, visible from the plains that characterise central Cylostra.

The peak's location placed it southeast of the Imperial heartland and northwest of [[Amberlight]], creating a natural geographical barrier that influenced regional military strategy during the war. The challenging terrain surrounding the mountain contributed to Amberlight's ability to maintain neutrality through much of the conflict, as the natural barriers deterred direct military confrontation.

### Post-war transformation

The firing of the [[Arcane Pulse Cannon]] during the Battle of the Thousand fundamentally reshaped the mountain's geography. The concentrated magical energy shattered the summit upon impact, triggering massive tremors felt throughout Cylostra. The resulting explosion sent enormous debris and molten material cascading primarily onto the advancing Imperial Army positioned to the north, whilst the mountain itself collapsed into a dramatically altered configuration.

The destruction created a circular plateau approximately three miles in diameter where the summit once stood, surrounded by three lower peaks that formed from the mountain's fragmented remains. This new geological formation, collectively designated as the Exalted Peaks, presents a unique landscape unlike any other mountain range in Cylostra.

### Current geography

The central plateau, flattened by the arcane devastation, rises organically from the mountain's base to create a dramatic multi-tiered landscape. The surrounding slopes descend in natural terraces that have been further developed and reinforced to support [[Geldeon]]'s tiered urban architecture. The three lower peaks that encircle the plateau vary in elevation and character, their surfaces bearing the visible scars of their violent formation.

The wasteland surrounding the southern base of the Exalted Peaks serves as an enduring reminder of the war's devastation, a barren expanse where the once, fertile lands were scorched by the weapon's discharge and the fallout from the mountain's destruction. This desolate ring separates the peaks from the recovering regions of Cylostra, creating a natural buffer that reinforces Geldeon's geographical isolation.

From its height, the [[Saphra River]] used to emerge naturally from the zenith. Now it is currently being maintained through magical sources as to avoid the downstream valley to collapse.

The molten and magically infused rock flowing hot after the Arcane Pulse Cannon's blast formed a downward path at the northern flank of the mountain known as the [[Obsidian Path]] due to its black and vitreous aspect.

## Historical significance

### The Battle of the Thousand

The Exalted Peak became the focal point of the Cylostran Civil War's climactic confrontation. Imperial forces advancing from the north and Lyranthian forces approaching from the south converged on the mountain, surrounding it in a massive pincer movement. The battle's name derives from the thousand-strong elite forces that clashed in the shadow of the mountain, whilst tens of thousands of regular troops massed in the surrounding terrain.

[[Lyranthe]]'s deployment of the Arcane Pulse Cannon, a weapon of unprecedented destructive capability, targeted the mountain's summit with the strategic intention of using the resulting debris to devastate the Imperial Army. The weapon fired only once, and the consequences far exceeded all tactical calculations. The concentrated magical energy not only shattered the mountain but triggered a catastrophic chain reaction that devastated both military forces and the nearby civilian population of Amberlight.

The mountain's destruction marked the definitive end of the unified Cylostran Empire, creating a geographical monument to the war's devastating conclusion that remains visible across the continent.

## Economic importance

### Mining operations

The violent transformation of the Exalted Peaks exposed valuable mineral deposits previously inaccessible within the mountain's depths. The fractured geology created by the arcane explosion revealed and created seams of precious metals, rare crystals, and other resources that have become economically significant to Geldeon's closed economy.

Extensive mining claims throughout the Exalted Peaks are controlled primarily by the [[Prenyan Houses]], particularly House Gruhor, which maintains the most substantial mining operations in the range. These claims represent considerable wealth and strategic resources, as the minerals extracted support Geldeon's manufacturing, arcane research, and trade activities within the city's self-sufficient economic system.

The mining operations concentrate on the three lower peaks rather than the inhabited central plateau, with carefully managed extraction that balances resource exploitation against the stability of Geldeon's foundation. The exposed geology continues to yield discoveries, as miners work through layers of rock transformed by the arcane energies that destroyed the original peak.

### Strategic resources

The resources extracted from the Exalted Peaks include both conventional minerals and materials imbued with residual arcane properties from the Arcane Pulse Cannon's discharge. These magically altered substances hold particular value for Geldeon's craftsmen and artificers, who have developed techniques to work with the unique properties of [[post-Rending materials]].

The strategic control of these resources reinforces the Prenyan Houses' economic dominance whilst contributing to Geldeon's ability to maintain technological and arcane sophistication without external trade dependencies.

## Settlement and development

### Foundation of Geldeon

Following the Battle of the Thousand, a small number of Amberlight survivors escaped the devastation, travelling northward to the destroyed mountain. These refugees, drawn by the plateau's defensible position and the psychological significance of building upon the weapon that had destroyed their home, established the settlement that would become Geldeon.

The flattened summit provided an unprecedented opportunity for urban planning, allowing the survivors to design a city that maximised the unique geography. The circular plateau and surrounding terraced slopes enabled the development of Geldeon's distinctive tiered architecture, with the most valuable real estate concentrated at the summit where the Prenyan Houses established their estates.

The three surrounding peaks provide natural defensive positions whilst the wasteland below creates a barrier that discourages external threats, contributing to Geldeon's isolation and security in the post-war era.

### Ongoing development

Geldeon's expansion has gradually extended from the central plateau down the mountain's slopes, following the natural terraces created by the destruction. The city's growth demonstrates careful planning that respects the mountain's altered geology whilst maximising the defensive and economic advantages of the vertical urban landscape.

The surrounding peaks remain largely undeveloped beyond mining infrastructure, preserving wilderness areas that serve both practical purposes, such as resource extraction and defensive monitoring, and symbolic functions as reminders of the natural world that existed before the Rending transformed the landscape.

## Cultural significance

The Exalted Peaks stand as Cylostra's most visible monument to the Cylostran Civil War, a permanent geographical alteration that serves as a constant reminder of the conflict's devastating conclusion. The transformation from a single majestic mountain to a shattered range embodies the broader fragmentation of the unified empire into competing city-states.

For Geldeon's inhabitants, particularly those descended from Amberlight's survivors, the peaks represent both loss and resilience. The mountain that was destroyed by the weapon built to defend their original home became the foundation for their new city, creating a complex cultural relationship with the landscape they inhabit.

The visible scars on the peaks' surfaces, the unnatural geometry of the central plateau, and the barren wasteland below serve as perpetual testimonies to the destructive capability of arcane warfare, influencing Geldeon's approach to magic, military power, and international relations in the Current Era.
