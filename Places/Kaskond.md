# Kaskond: The Waning Goliath

## Overview

Kaskond stands as the oldest city in [[Cylostra|Cylostra]], once the imperial capital and self-proclaimed heart of the Cylostran Empire before [[The Cylostran Civil War|the Rending]]. Though its glory has faded in the decades following the war, the city maintains a stubborn resilience, refusing to completely surrender its position as a significant power in the post-war landscape.

## History and Legacy

As the former capital of the unified Cylostran Empire, Kaskond once commanded vast territories across the continent. The city amassed extraordinary wealth through exploitative practices imposed on its provinces, extracting resources and tribute from territories spanning from the northern deserts to the southern coasts.

> "The walls of Kaskond were built with gold wrung from a hundred conquered peoples, and the streets were paved with the broken promises of a thousand subjugated villages." 
> 
> — Common saying in the outer provinces

### Imperial Authority

During the height of imperial power, Kaskond served as the administrative centre from which the Emperor controlled the vast Cylostran territories. The imperial court was notorious for its:

* Elaborate bureaucracy designed to maintain control over distant regions
* Harsh tax collection systems that funnelled wealth to the capital
* Punitive measures against rebellious provinces
* Lavish displays of wealth and power to intimidate foreign dignitaries

Cruelty was a staple of the empire, with public executions and demonstrations of imperial might serving as common methods to quell dissent and maintain order across the realm.

## Military Prowess

### The Imperial Army

Before the Rending, Kaskond commanded the largest standing army in Cylostra, with garrisons positioned throughout the empire to maintain control and expand imperial borders when opportunities arose.

### The Emperor's Talons

The elite intelligence organization known as the Emperor's Talons operated from Kaskond, serving as the empire's most feared weapon against both internal and external threats. These specialized operatives excelled in:

* Espionage and counter-intelligence
* Targeted assassination of enemy leadership
* Sabotage of rival infrastructure
* Fostering civil unrest in opposing territories

The Talons operated directly under imperial authority, receiving funding and directives from the highest levels of government.

## Present Condition

### Economic Decline

In the three decades since the Cylostran Civil War, Kaskond has experienced a steady economic decline. Without tribute from conquered territories and with trade routes disrupted by new political boundaries, the city's once-overflowing coffers have gradually emptied.
Despite this diminished status, Kaskond refuses to acknowledge its reduced circumstances, maintaining an expensive façade of imperial grandeur even as its infrastructure crumbles beneath the surface.

### Political Structure

Following the fracturing of the empire, Kaskond has transitioned to a merchant republic where:

* Voting rights are tied to guild membership
* Former imperial officials struggle to maintain influence
* Military leadership continues to hold significant sway in governance
* Economic interests increasingly dictate policy decisions

### Relations with Other Cities

Kaskond maintains a cold, distrustful stance toward both Geldeon and Lyranthe, viewing the former as usurpers of imperial prestige and the latter as treasonous rebels. Diplomatic relations exist but remain strained by the lingering bitterness of defeat and the ongoing competition for resources and influence in post-war Cylostra.

## Architecture and Layout

The city's architecture reflects its imperial past, featuring:

* Imposing government buildings designed to showcase authority
* Ancient defensive walls and watchtowers surrounding the central districts
* Palaces and estates of former imperial officials, many now repurposed
* Grand avenues designed for military parades, now serving commercial purposes
* Monuments commemorating imperial conquests, some damaged during the war

## Current Challenges

Kaskond faces numerous difficulties in the post-war era:

* Adapting its economy from one based on tribute to one based on production and trade
* Maintaining expensive imperial infrastructure with diminished resources
* Managing the expectations of a population raised on promises of inherent superiority
* Preventing the complete collapse of its international standing and influence

Despite these challenges, the city clings tenaciously to its heritage and refuses to accept a diminished role in Cylostra's future, continually seeking ways to reclaim its former glory and influence.
