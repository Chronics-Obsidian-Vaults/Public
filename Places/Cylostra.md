# The Region of Cylostra

Cylostra encompasses the largest continental landmass on the globe, a sprawling territory that stretches from the frigid southwestern tundra to the harsh arid sands of the north. In addition to the main continent, several significant islands dot the surrounding seas, each with their own unique cultures and ecosystems.

### Geography and Climate

The heart of Cylostra consists of vast, rolling plains that extend for thousands of miles. These grasslands, known locally as the [[Alderian Expanse]], are characterized by tall, golden grasses that wave like oceans under the wind. The plains are neighboured by an imposing mountain range called the [[Exalted Peaks]], whose summits can be seen from hundreds of miles away on clear days adorned with the imposing architecture of the area.

The western regions feature dense deciduous forests that gradually transition into coniferous woodlands as the elevation increases known as the [[Avourel Tundra]]. To the east, the terrain gives way to rocky bad-lands and eventually a coastal region with dramatic cliffs and sheltered bays. The southern border is defined by the [[Everfrost Sea]] that serves as natural boundaries between Cylostra and neighbouring territories.
The climate varies dramatically across the region, with harsh winters in the south, moderate conditions in the central plains, and subtropical warmth in the northern reaches. This diversity has contributed to the rich tapestry of flora and fauna that call Cylostra home.


### History and Civilization

Cylostra's human history spans back thousands of years, with evidence of early settlements dating to the First Light. As civilization developed, distinct city-states emerged, each with their own governing philosophies, architectural styles, and cultural practices.
The region witnessed a devastating civil war known as [[The Cylostran Civil War|the Rending]] that lasted for nearly three centuries. As per official report, this brutal conflict began as a dispute over water rights between the northern and southern states but quickly escalated into a full-scale war that engulfed the entire region. Records speak of fields turned to ash, rivers running red with blood, and entire cities reduced to rubble.
The war ended approximately thirty years ago after the Battle of the Thousand, where the combined losses were so catastrophic that the battlefield is still a barren wasteland. Almost no one dares enter Amberlight. The details are unknown to many as why every party involved suddenly stopped fighting.

### Current State

Though the scars of war are still visible on the landscape—abandoned fortifications, memorial sites, and the occasional unexploded arcane device—Cylostra is now in a period of healing and rebuilding. The once-scorched earth is gradually recovering, with new growth covering battlefields and wildlife returning to areas that were once abandoned.
The city-states have developed unique approaches to governance in the post-war era. [[Geldeon|Geldeon]], the largest city, has embraced closed council strategy where their members are completely anonymous to everyone. The southern metropolis of [[Lyranthe|Lyranthe]] operates under a council of elected elders who serve five-year terms. The central trading hub of [[Kaskond|Kaskond]] maintains a merchant republic where voting rights are tied to guild membership.
Tensions still simmer beneath the surface. Resource disputes occasionally flare up, particularly in years of drought when water becomes scarce.
Geldeon was the better of the three, setting itself as the de facto powerhouse across Cylostra.
